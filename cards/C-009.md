## Scope

`AdvisoryRecord` plus optional `PatchRecord` and changelog text into SAST-oriented

provenance-backed `SecurityPatternRecord` objects without inventing version ranges,
and atomically export deterministic `security_patterns.jsonl`. Do not add KB documents,
